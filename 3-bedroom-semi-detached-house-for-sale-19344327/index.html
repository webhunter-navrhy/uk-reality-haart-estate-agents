Description

Guide Price £150,000 - £170,000

Situated on Nocton Drive in Lincoln, this three-bedroom semi-detached home offers a practical layout, off-road parking, and clear potential for improvement, making it well suited to first-time buyers or investors.

The property is positioned on a quiet residential street and benefits from a driveway to the front, along with a low-maintenance garden area.

Internally, an entrance porch leads into the hallway with stairs to the first floor. The main lounge runs from front to rear, providing a functional living space with good natural light.

To the rear, the kitchen sits across the back of the property and gives access out to the garden. An internal side passage leads through to a useful utility/work area, along with a ground floor shower room fitted with a three-piece suite including walk-in shower.

Upstairs, there are two double bedrooms and a third smaller single bedroom, best suited as a home office, nursery, or dressing room.

Externally, the rear garden offers a mix of paved and planted areas, with scope to improve and reconfigure to suit.

The property would benefit from modernisation and general improvement throughout, however it offers a solid base for buyers looking to add value and personalise.

Nocton Drive is conveniently located for access to Lincoln city centre, with local shops, supermarkets, schooling, and transport links all within easy reach.
